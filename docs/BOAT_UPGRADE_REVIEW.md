## Delivered assets and game changes

- Original reference sheet and exact image generation prompt: `assets/references/README.md` and `ocean-boats-v2-reference.png`. Generated with the built-in Imagegen tool; no exact selectable image-model version was exposed.
- Editable full and blockout Blender scenes under `assets/blender/`; repeatable builders under `scripts/`.
- Aurora GLB: `public/models/aurora_explorer_yacht_v2.glb`, 6,914,992 bytes; 18 exported meshes and 38,756 Blender vertices.
- Voyager GLB: `public/models/voyager_research_launch_v2.glb`, 5,470,092 bytes; 18 exported meshes and 27,257 Blender vertices.
- Fitted curved hulls, real window apertures, divided panes and doors, interior helm geometry, teak material maps, supported seating, rails, rubber fenders, dive stairs/platform, tanks, sample equipment, radar, and Aurora crane.
- Browser finishing: gelcoat tuning, reflected sea/shore on cabin glazing, modest Quality transmission and interior light, hull waterline patina, vessel-following shadows, and six camera views including a close working-deck view.
- Aurora's final upper stair stringers and solid observation tabletop are generated by `addVesselFinishDetails` in `src/main.ts`. Preserve this code alongside the GLBs: exporting the bare GLBs does not reproduce the entire reviewed result.
- Corrected boat and swimmer facing direction, left/right steering, reverse rudder behavior, acceleration/drag/braking, stationary drift, per-vessel collision dimensions, wake, and diver launch placement.
- Boat switching prevents duplicate actions, loads before charging/unlocking, restores the previous boat on failure, and disposes replaced mesh resources.
- Dive camera snaps below the surface, underwater fog and overlay are lighter, and music requires an explicit user action.

## Practical limits and next work

Quality screenshot HUD readings ranged roughly from 24 to 41 fps during review; these are snapshots, not sustained benchmarks. Balanced is the default. Quality uses shadows, glass transmission and occasional reflection captures, which add rendering cost. Real mobile performance, sustained helm controls, slow docking, exact collision contacts and complete contract playthroughs remain unverified. Hull collision is still an approximate cuboid rather than a detailed collision mesh. The GLBs should receive compression and distance detail levels before claiming phone readiness.

The surrounding harbor, islands and underwater environment still contain prototype geometry. No new reef survey, species photography, sampling tools, authored wreck mission, full expedition save system, advertising, CrateShip integration or publication was completed by this boat pass.

Next: finish and playtest movement/collision; author one reef; implement photography, sampling and journal; add the compact wreck recovery and harbor debrief; then verify a complete saved chapter. The proposed demo is 15 to 20 minutes, subject to playtesting. See `OCEAN_DEMO_UPGRADE_PLAN.md` for the complete plan.

The preview serves `dist`. Run `npm run build` after every source or GLB change before reloading the preview.
